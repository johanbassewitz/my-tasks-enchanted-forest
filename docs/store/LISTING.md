# Daywhisper — Tasks & Planner

## Short description
A calmer space for your tasks, calendar and focused days. Core planning stays on your PC.

## Description
Make room for what matters with Daywhisper, a cozy Windows planner built for small,
steady steps. Organize tasks and events, break projects into phases, add subtasks,
and take a quiet moment with the focus timer.

The core planner is free: recurring tasks, a daily/weekly/monthly calendar, local
reminders, search, trash restoration, automatic local backups and manual export.
No Google account or calendar connection is required.

Choose the included Enchanted Forest theme, or explore Midnight Observatory and
Amber Hearth. Optional packs add study planning, a creator pipeline, freelance
project tracking, habits and journaling. Paid packs are separate permanent purchases.
Supporter subscriptions remove sponsored placements and provide premium theme access
while subscribed. Individually purchased themes remain available after cancellation.

Local Windows reminders work while Daywhisper is open, minimized or in the tray.
They do not alert while the application is fully quit or the PC is off. Leave
Daywhisper running in the tray for reminders. No mobile application or device sync
is included.
